Aurora Grid

Distributed frame rendering across independent GPUs.

Aurora Grid splits a single render across a mesh of machines and reassembles it before the next refresh, using a predictive scheduler to rebalance load mid-frame.

Quiet Mesh

Peer-to-peer messaging that survives dead networks.

Messages hop device-to-device over local radio when the internet drops, re-encrypting at every hop, then reconcile with the cloud once connectivity returns.
