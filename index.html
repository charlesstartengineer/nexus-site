What we build

Software for the layer that hasn’t shipped yet.

Every NEXUS project starts as an internal experiment: a rendering trick, a protocol idea, a strange interaction that shouldn’t work but does. The ones that survive scrutiny get turned into products — and the ones that don’t become write-ups on the Lab page.

We work in small, focused teams of engineers and designers who ship together, rather than handing work down a pipeline.
