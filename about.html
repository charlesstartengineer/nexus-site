NEXUS Research Studio — Est. 2019

About NEXUS

We build the parts of the future that need to work today.

NEXUS started as three engineers trading prototypes over a group chat. It's now a studio of thirty, still shipping the same way — small teams, working software, no long roadmaps.

Our mission

Make the next layer of computing feel inevitable, not futuristic.

Most "future of the internet" work stays a demo. We measure a project by whether someone outside the studio depends on it a year later — not by how it looks in a reel.

That means real infrastructure, real edge cases, and a willingness to kill an idea the moment it stops holding up under load.

Timeline

How we got here.

2019

Three engineers, one repo

NEXUS begins as a private repository for weekend experiments — mostly rendering tricks and networking oddities with no product in mind.

2021

First external release

Signal Atlas ships to a handful of city planning teams. It's rough, but it's the first project anyone outside the studio relies on.

2023

The studio forms properly

NEXUS becomes a company of twelve. The Lab page goes public so the experiments that don't become products still get shared.

2026

Six live products, one studio

Thirty people across systems, design and research, still shipping in the same small-team shape we started with.

Values

What we hold each other to.

01

Ship the ugly version first

A working prototype teaches us more in a day than a polished spec teaches us in a month.

02

Own it past the demo

Whoever builds a system keeps carrying it through the boring parts: monitoring, edge cases, 2am pages.

03

Publish the failures too

The Lab exists because a documented dead end saves the next person months.
